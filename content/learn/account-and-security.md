# Account, security and privacy

## Keep your account safe
- Use a unique password of at least 10 characters. A password manager makes this easy.
- Never share your password or API keys. AiWazz staff will never ask for them.
- Sign out on shared computers (avatar menu → **Sign out**).

## API keys
- Create separate keys for each app or script, with clear names.
- If a key might be exposed (for example pushed to GitHub), **revoke it** in **API keys** and create a new one.

## What happens to your data
- **Audio files** are deleted automatically after 7 days.
- **Scripts** stay in your history until you delete them.
- **We never use your scripts or audio to train AI models**, and we never sell your data.
- Full details: [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

## Change your email or password
**Settings → Account.** You'll need your current password.

## Delete your account
**Settings → Account → Delete account**, or follow the [Data Deletion instructions](/data-deletion/). Deletion is permanent.

## Report misuse
If you think someone used AiWazz to imitate you or cause harm, contact our [Grievance Officer](/grievance/).
