# Glossary

- **TTS (text to speech):** technology that turns written text into spoken audio.
- **Voice:** a synthetic speaker with its own tone, gender and accent.
- **Character:** one letter, number, space or punctuation mark in your script; how usage is counted.
- **Generation / job:** one request to turn a script into audio.
- **Queue:** the waiting line of jobs; AiWazz processes them one after another.
- **Speed:** how fast the voice talks (0.7–1.3, 1.0 is natural).
- **LUFS:** a measure of perceived loudness. Most short-video platforms target around -14 LUFS.
- **True peak (dBTP):** the highest point of the audio wave. Keeping it below -1 dBTP prevents distortion after upload.
- **Sample rate (48 kHz):** how many times per second the audio is measured. 48 kHz is the standard for video.
- **MP3:** compressed audio: small files, very good quality.
- **WAV:** uncompressed audio: large files, best quality for editing.
- **Devanagari:** the script used to write Hindi (हिंदी).
- **Hinglish:** a mix of Hindi and English in one sentence.
- **Transliteration:** writing a word in a different script without translating it (namaste → नमस्ते).
- **API:** a way for software to use AiWazz directly, without the website.
- **API key:** a secret code that identifies your account when using the API.
- **Ducking:** automatically lowering music when the voice speaks.
