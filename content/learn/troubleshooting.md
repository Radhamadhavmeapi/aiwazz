# Troubleshooting

## The Hindi voice sounds like it's reading English
Your Hindi is probably in Roman letters ("aaj hum…"). Write it in Devanagari. See the [Hindi and Hinglish guide](/learn/hindi-and-hinglish/).

## A word is mispronounced
Spell it the way it sounds, write Hindi words in Devanagari, add commas around it, or try another voice. Write numbers and abbreviations in words. More in [Write scripts that sound natural](/learn/writing-scripts/).

## Generation is taking a long time
Generations run in a queue. A 60-second voiceover usually takes about a minute once it starts. If you see a queue position, other jobs are ahead of you. Long scripts: split them into scenes. If a job seems stuck for more than 10 minutes, refresh the page; failed jobs never use your characters.

## "Not enough characters"
You've used this period's characters. Upgrade in **Settings → Billing**, or wait for your plan to renew.

## The audio is too quiet or too loud in my video
Keep loudness levelling on (default -14 LUFS). In your editor, check you haven't lowered the voice track, and keep background music 15–20 dB below the voice.

## I can't find an old file
Audio is kept for 7 days. After that, generate it again from History (your script is saved).

## Download doesn't start on my phone
Long-press the download button and choose **Download link**, or open the dashboard in Chrome or Safari instead of an in-app browser (Instagram/WhatsApp).

## I can't sign in
Check your email address for typos and use **Forgot password**. After several wrong attempts, sign-in is paused for a few minutes for your safety.

## Payment went through but my plan didn't change
Wait 2–3 minutes and refresh. If it's still not updated after 24 hours, email support with your Razorpay payment ID (starts with `pay_`). See our [Shipping and Delivery Policy](/shipping-policy/).
