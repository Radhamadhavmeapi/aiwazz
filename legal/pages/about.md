# About AiWazz

**Type it, hear it.**

AiWazz is an AI text to speech platform that turns written scripts into natural, ready-to-publish voiceovers in Hindi, Hinglish and English. Creators, small businesses, teachers and agencies use it to voice reels, YouTube videos, ads, explainers and training content in minutes instead of booking a studio.

## What we offer

- **Natural voices:** male and female voices in Hindi (Devanagari) and English (US and UK accents).
- **Platform-ready audio:** every file is trimmed and loudness-levelled, and delivered as MP3 and WAV at 48 kHz.
- **Simple workflow:** paste a script, choose a voice, press Generate, download.
- **API access:** developers and agencies can generate voiceovers from their own tools on paid plans.
