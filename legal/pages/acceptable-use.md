# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective date:** {{effectiveDate}} · **Last updated:** {{lastUpdated}}

AI voices are powerful. This policy explains what you may not do with AiWazz. It is part of our [Terms and Conditions](/terms/).

## You must not use AiWazz to:

1. **Impersonate real people:** create audio that pretends to be, or could reasonably be mistaken for, a real person (politicians, celebrities, officials, customers, family members) without their documented consent.
2. **Deceive or defraud:** run scams, fake customer-care or bank calls, OTP or KYC fraud, phishing, fake emergencies, or any content meant to trick people into sending money or information.
3. **Spread misinformation:** create fake news, false statements attributed to real people or organisations, or content meant to mislead voters or interfere with elections.
4. **Harass or harm:** create threatening, abusive, defamatory, hateful or discriminatory content, or content that promotes violence, terrorism or self-harm.
5. **Create sexual content involving minors** or any non-consensual sexual content.
6. **Infringe rights:** voice copyrighted text you have no right to use (for example whole books or song lyrics), or breach trademarks, privacy or other rights.
7. **Send spam:** produce automated calls or messages to people who have not agreed to receive them, or breach telecom rules (TRAI regulations) or the policies of messaging platforms such as WhatsApp.
8. **Break the law:** create anything illegal under Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, or the laws where you or your audience are.
9. **Abuse the Service:** share or resell accounts or API keys without permission, get around usage limits, scrape, reverse-engineer, overload or attack the Service, or use it to build a competing voice dataset or model.

## Label AI-generated audio

Where the law or a platform's rules require AI-generated or synthetic content to be labelled or disclosed, you must do so. We recommend telling your audience when a voice is AI-generated, especially in ads, news-like or political content.

## What we do about misuse

We may remove content, limit features, suspend or close accounts without refund, keep relevant records, and report illegal activity to law enforcement. We may review requests and outputs when we receive a complaint or detect abuse.

## Report misuse

If you believe AiWazz audio is being used to impersonate you or to cause harm, contact our Grievance Officer at {{grievanceOfficer.email}} with links or files and details. We act on valid reports within 24 to 72 hours.
