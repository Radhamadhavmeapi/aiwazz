## How to raise a grievance

Email the Grievance Officer with:

1. your name and registered email address (if you have an account),
2. what the complaint is about: content or misuse, privacy, payment, or account,
3. links, files, payment IDs or screenshots that help us investigate.

## Timelines

- **Acknowledgement:** within 24 hours.
- **Resolution:** within 15 days of receipt.
- **Urgent requests** about impersonation, non-consensual or sexual content are acted on within 24 to 72 hours.

If you are not satisfied with our response to a personal-data complaint, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
