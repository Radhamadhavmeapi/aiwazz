## What is a character?

Every letter, number, space and punctuation mark in your script counts as one character, in any language. A typical 60-second reel script is about 700 to 900 characters.

## Billing details

- All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR). Applicable GST ({{gstRatePercent}}%) is added at checkout, and a GST invoice is issued for every payment.
- Payments are processed securely by Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking and wallets). AiWazz never sees or stores your full card details.
- Plans renew automatically at the end of each billing period until you cancel. You can cancel any time from **Settings → Billing**; your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
- Unused characters do not roll over to the next billing period.
- If a generation fails on our side, the characters are returned to your balance automatically.
- Refunds are covered by our [Cancellation and Refund Policy](/refund-policy/).

## Commercial use

Paid plans include commercial rights to the audio you generate: ads, client projects, monetised YouTube and social media. See our [Terms and Conditions](/terms/). The Free plan is for personal, non-commercial use.

**Is there a free plan?** Yes. The Free plan gives you {{plans.0.characters}} characters a month to try every voice.

**Can I change plans?** Yes. Upgrades apply immediately, with the price difference charged pro rata. Downgrades take effect from the next billing period.

**Do you offer refunds?** Yes, in the cases listed in our [Cancellation and Refund Policy](/refund-policy/).
