# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** {{effectiveDate}} · **Last updated:** {{lastUpdated}}

This Privacy Policy explains how **{{legalName}}** (GSTIN {{gstin}}), {{address}} ("AiWazz", "we", "us"), collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use {{websites.product}}, {{websites.app}}, the AiWazz API and related services (the "Service"). We act as the Data Fiduciary for this data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

## 1. Data we collect

| Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | Name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), invite code | You, at signup |
| Usage content | Scripts (text) you submit, the audio files generated from them, and the words in your pronunciation list (deleted with your account) | You, when you use the Service |
| Billing data | Plan, amount, GSTIN and billing name (if you provide them), Razorpay payment and order IDs, payment status | You and Razorpay |
| Technical data | IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, timestamps, error logs | Automatically, when you use the Service |
| Support data | Messages you send by email, WhatsApp or the contact form | You |
| Invite requests | Email address, optional name and what you plan to make, IP address | You, when you request an invite |
| API data | API key identifiers (keys are stored hashed) and request logs | Automatically |

We do **not** collect your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking password. These are entered on Razorpay's secure checkout and processed by Razorpay.

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 2. Why we use your data

- To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
- To convert your scripts into audio and deliver the files to you.
- To process payments, issue GST invoices and keep tax and accounting records.
- To send service emails: receipts, password resets, security alerts and policy changes.
- To provide support and handle complaints.
- To send you an invite code if you asked for one during early access.
- To prevent fraud, abuse and violations of our [Acceptable Use Policy](/acceptable-use/).
- To understand and improve the Service using aggregated, non-identifying statistics.
- To send product updates or offers, **only** if you opt in. You can unsubscribe at any time.

We process your data based on your consent, to perform our contract with you, and to meet legal obligations, as permitted by the DPDP Act and other applicable law. **We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your scripts or audio to train AI models.**

## 3. How long we keep data

- **Generated audio:** deleted automatically {{audioRetentionDays}} days after it is created.
- **Scripts (text):** kept with your generation history until you delete them or your account.
- **Account data:** kept while your account is active, and deleted within 30 days after you delete your account.
- **Billing and invoice records:** kept for 8 years, as required by Indian tax and accounting laws.
- **Security and server logs:** kept for up to 90 days.
- **Invite requests:** kept until we send you an invite, and deleted automatically 12 months after your last request, or sooner if you ask us.

## 4. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with service providers who help us run the Service, under contracts that require them to protect it:

- **Razorpay Software Private Limited:** payment processing.
- **Hosting provider:** our servers, which store the Service's data.
- **Email:** we send and receive email on our own mail server at the hosting provider above; no separate email service receives your data.
- **Authorities:** when required by law, a court order or a lawful government request, or to protect rights and safety.

If you sign in with or connect a third-party service (for example Google or Meta) in future, we receive only the data you authorise, such as your name and email, and use it only to sign you in. We do not share data received from Meta platforms with data brokers or use it for advertising.

## 5. Cookies

We use only essential cookies, which keep you signed in and protect forms against misuse, and a cookie that remembers your light or dark theme. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

## 6. Security

We use HTTPS encryption, hashed passwords and API keys, access controls, firewalls and regular backups. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India of a personal data breach as required by law.

## 7. Where data is stored

Your data is stored on servers we control. If we use providers outside India, we will do so only as permitted by Indian law.

## 8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act and other applicable law, you can:

- **Access** a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct or update** inaccurate data (most of it directly in Settings).
- **Delete** your data and account. See [Data Deletion](/data-deletion/) for the steps.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time. This does not affect processing already done, and some features may stop working.
- **Nominate** another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated.
- **Raise a grievance** with us and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, email {{privacyEmail}} from your registered email address. We respond within 30 days.

## 9. How to request deletion of your data

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time:

1. **In the app:** sign in at {{websites.app}} → Settings → Account → **Delete account**.
2. **By email:** write to {{privacyEmail}} from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my data".

We confirm deletion within 30 days. Billing records we must keep by law are retained only for that purpose. Full steps: [Data Deletion Instructions](/data-deletion/).

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new "Last updated" date and, for important changes, notify you by email or in the app.
