# Cancellation and Refund Policy

**Effective date:** {{effectiveDate}} · **Last updated:** {{lastUpdated}}

This policy applies to all purchases of AiWazz plans from **{{legalName}}** ({{brand}}). AiWazz is a digital service, and no physical goods are sold.

## 1. Cancelling your subscription

- You can cancel any time from **Settings → Billing → Cancel plan** at {{websites.app}}, or by emailing {{supportEmail}} from your registered email address.
- Cancelling stops automatic renewal. Your plan stays active, with its remaining characters, until the end of the billing period you have paid for. After that, your account moves to the Free plan.
- No cancellation fee is charged.

## 2. When you can get a refund

| Situation | Refund |
|---|---|
| **First purchase of a paid plan**, requested within **7 days** of payment, with less than **10%** of the plan's characters used | Full refund |
| **Charged twice** for the same order, or charged when the payment showed as failed | Full refund of the extra or failed amount |
| **Service unavailable** because of a fault on our side for more than 72 hours in a row during your billing period | Pro-rata refund or service credit, as you choose |
| **Generation failed** because of an error on our side | Characters returned to your balance automatically |
| Renewal charged after you asked to cancel **before** the renewal date | Full refund of that renewal |

## 3. When refunds are not given

- Renewals, monthly or yearly, once the new billing period has started, unless covered in section 2.
- Plans where 10% or more of the characters have been used.
- Change of mind after 7 days from the first purchase.
- Accounts suspended or terminated for breaching our [Terms](/terms/) or [Acceptable Use Policy](/acceptable-use/).
- Unused characters at the end of a billing period.

## 4. How to request a refund

Email {{supportEmail}} with:

- your registered email address,
- the Razorpay payment ID (starts with `pay_`, shown on your receipt), and
- the reason for the request.

We reply within 2 business days to approve or decline the request.

## 5. Refund timeline and method

- Approved refunds are initiated within **2 business days** of approval.
- Refunds are made to the **original payment method** (UPI, card, net banking or wallet) through Razorpay.
- The amount usually reaches your account within **5 to 7 business days** after it is initiated, depending on your bank.
- The GST invoice is adjusted with a credit note.
