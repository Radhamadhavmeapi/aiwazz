# Shipping and Delivery Policy

**Effective date:** {{effectiveDate}} · **Last updated:** {{lastUpdated}}

AiWazz, by **{{legalName}}**, is an online software service. **We do not ship any physical products**, and no shipping charges apply.

## How your purchase is delivered

- **Plan activation:** your plan is activated on your AiWazz account **immediately** after a successful payment, usually within a few seconds and always within 24 hours.
- **Confirmation:** you receive a payment confirmation and GST invoice at your registered email address.
- **Access:** you use the Service online at {{websites.app}}, from any modern browser in India or abroad.
- **Audio delivery:** each voiceover is delivered in your dashboard as MP3 and WAV files to download, typically within a few minutes depending on script length and queue. Files stay available for {{audioRetentionDays}} days.
- **API:** on plans with API access, audio is returned through the API.

## If your plan is not activated

If your payment succeeded but your plan is not active within 24 hours, email {{supportEmail}} or call {{phone}} with your registered email address and Razorpay payment ID. We will activate your plan or refund you in full, as described in our [Cancellation and Refund Policy](/refund-policy/).

## Service area

The Service is available online worldwide. Prices are charged in Indian Rupees (INR).
